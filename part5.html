🛑 Next Day

Agla din subah se hi Gokuldham Society mein kuch alag mahoul tha. Rukmini kaam kar rahi thi lekin uske chehre par udaasi saaf dikhai de rahi thi. Daya aur Anjali ne usko dekha toh dono ko samajh aa gaya ki kuch gadbad hai.

Daya

"Arre Rukmini, aaj tera chehra kyun itna udaas hai? Bata na kya baat hai?"

Rukmini thodi si muskurayi lekin kuch nahi boli. Madhavi ke ghar pahunch kar bhi woh chup-chaap kaam karti rahi. Uski moti gaand pochha lagate waqt hil rahi thi, lekin aaj usme woh purani chamak nahi thi.

Shaam hote-hote Rukmini ne notice board par ek chhoti si notice likhwa di. Bhide ne likhte waqt bhi uski taraf dekha aur halke se poocha, lekin Rukmini ne sirf bola ki sab mahilayein kal subah garden mein milen.

⚡   GARDEN MEIN EMERGENCY MEETING   ⚡
🌿 Garden — Emergency Meeting

Subah garden mein saari mahilayein ikatthi ho gayi thi — Daya, Madhavi, Anjali, Babita, Komal, Roshan sab. Sabki tension dikhai de rahi thi. Rukmini aakhri baar kaam karne aayi thi, lekin uska chehra serious tha.

Daya

"Arre Rukmini, tu mazak kar rahi hai na? Tu kal se kaam nahi aayegi?"

Rukmini

"Nahin bhabhi ji... main sach bol rahi hoon. Kal se main kaam nahi aaungi."

Saari mahilayein ek saath chillayi. Madhavi ne aage badh kar Rukmini ke haath pakde.

Madhavi

"Arre Rukmini, problem kya hai bata na. Hum sab milkar solve kar denge. Pagar badha denge, zyada aaram de denge... tu jo bhi maangegi wo dungi. Tera yeh sharir aur mehnat humare bina kaise chalega?"

Anjali

"Haan Rukmini, kuch bhi chahiye ho toh bol. Hum sab tujhe bahut pyar se rakh sakte hain... bahut achhe se."

Komal

"Agar thak jaaye toh AC room mein aaram kar lena... hum sab tere saath rahenge."

Rukmini ki aankhon mein aansu aa gaye. Woh dheere se boli:

Rukmini

"Bhabhi ji... mere pati ko uske seth ne naukri se nikaal diya hai. Ab sirf mere ek salary se Mumbai mein ghar kaise chalega? Hum gaon laut jaayenge."

Saari mahilayein shocked ho gayi. Daya ne usko gale laga liya.

Daya

"Arre nahin Rukmini... tu humein chhod kar mat ja. Teri bina hum sab ka ghar adhura reh jaayega. Hum tere pati ke liye bhi kuch arrangement kar denge... bas tu mat ja."

Babita

"Haan, hum sab milkar tujhe itna khush rakh sakte hain ki tu kabhi nahi jaayegi."

Rukmini ne sabko namaste kiya aur halke se muskurate hue boli:

Rukmini

"Aap sab bahut achhi ho. Mujhe bhi aap sabki bahut yaad aayegi... lekin ab jaana padega."

Saari mahilayein Rukmini ko rokne ki koshish karti rahi — kuch uske haath pakad rahi thi, kuch uske kandhe par haath fer rahi thi. Garden mein pura mahoul udaasi aur garam pyaas se bhara hua tha. Rukmini jaate waqt ek baar phir sabki taraf dekhi, uski aankhon mein bhi dard tha.

✦   GHAR MEIN   ✦

Jethalal dukaan se thak kar ghar aaya toh pura ghar suna pada tha. Daya kitchen mein baithi ro rahi thi.

Daya

"Tapu ke papa! Rukmini humein chhod kar chali gayi! Ab mera kya hoga? Kaun ghar ka kaam karega?"

Jethalal ko sun kar jhatka laga. Champaklal bhi pareshan ho gaya. Daya ne bataya ki Rukmini ke pati ki naukri chali gayi isliye wo apne bacche ke saath gaon laut rahi hai.

Madhavi bilkul paagal ho rahi thi. Rukmini ke bina ghar ka saara kaam uske sar par aa gaya tha.

Madhavi

"Dekha aapne? Rukmini chali gayi aur ab mera pura din barbaad! Aapko certificate laane bheja tha, lekin aap..."

Bhide chup-chaap baitha tha. Madhavi ne phone kiya dusri bai ke liye, lekin koi nahi mila. Bhide ko bhi papad banana aur ghar sambhalna pad raha tha.

Anjali pura din phone lagaye ja rahi thi — saheliyon ko, relatives ko, sabko. Koi bai nahi mil rahi thi.

Anjali

"Taarak, Rukmini ke jaane ke baad pata chala life mein bai kitni zaroori hoti hai. Ab sab kaam mujhe khud karna pad raha hai!"

Taarak ne offer kiya ki woh help karega, lekin dono ko Rukmini ki bahut kami mehsoos ho rahi thi.

Shaam ko Abdul ke shop par sab mard ikatthe hue — Jethalal, Taarak, Bhide, Sodhi, Dr. Hathi. Sab Rukmini ke jaane ki baat kar rahe the. Husbands ne decide kiya ki ab unko apni biwiyon ki madad karni padegi. Sab has rahe the lekin andar se pareshan ho gaye the...!!!!!

  DAYA HOUSE — MAHILA MANDAL MEETING  
🏠 Daya House — Mahila Mandal Meeting

Daya ke ghar mein saari bhabhiyaan baithi thi. Rukmini ke jaane ke baad ghar ka mahoul bilkul udaas aur sunsaan ho gaya tha.

Daya

"Arre bhabhiyon, Rukmini chali gayi na... jaise mera pura din ruk gaya. Subah uth ke dekha toh ghar bilkul khali lag raha tha. Uski woh jaldi-jaldi kaam karne wali style... sab kuch sambhal leti thi. Ab mera sar chakr aa raha hai."

Madhavi

"Bilkul sahi keh rahi ho Daya behen. Mere ghar mein toh abhi tak kuch nahi hua. Rukmini jab pochha lagati thi na, toh lagta tha jaise pura floor chamak utha. Uski woh mehnat... woh paseena... bilkul alag level ki thi. Ab main khud kar rahi hoon toh thak jaati hoon."

Anjali

"Haan yaar, main bhi soch rahi thi. Rukmini jab kaam karti thi na, toh sab kuch ekdum perfect ho jaata tha. Ab ghar mein sab kuch adhura reh jaata hai... jaise kuch missing ho."

Babita

"Mere curtains abhi tak dhule nahi. Rukmini jab mere bedroom mein kaam karti thi na, toh pura room chamak uthata tha. Ab mujhe khud sab karna pad raha hai, thak jaati hoon."

Anjali

"Meri baat suniye. Aap logon ko pata hai kya hua? Maine mere saare friends ko phone kiya tha aur kaha tha kaamwali bhejein."

Roshan

"Wow! Usme se ek taiyaar bhi ho gai thi?"

Anjali

"Par jab use pata chala ki Gokuldham Society mein kaam karna hai, tab usne na bol diya!"

Babita

"Are how mean! Kyun?"

Anjali

"Kaha ki Gokuldham Society mein roz koi na koi problem shuru ho jaati hai."

Madhavi

"Ek minute, ek minute. Anjali bhabhi ekdam sahi bol rahi hain. Maine bhi padose ki society ki kaamwali ko jaasti pagar dene ki lalach di, lekin koi fayda nahin hua. Usne bhi mana kar diya."

Anjali

"Ek minute, ek minute. Aap itni chinta mat kijiye, koi na koi kaamwali hamein mil jayegi, I promise."

Madhavi

"Mein toh kehti hoon kaamwali bai jaldi mile na toh accha hai, warna roz-roz patiyon ki madad lenge na toh ghar mein jhagde hone lagenge."

Daya

"Nahin, is dukhne se kab aazadi milegi?"
