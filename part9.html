✦   STORY CONTINUES   ✦
After Jethalal & Lovely's Intense Bedroom Encounter

Jethalal aur Lovely ke bedroom mein zor-dar chudai ke baad dono thak kar let gaye the . Lovely Jethalal ke seene par haath ferte hue muskurayi aur boli ,

Lovely

"Jethaji... aap bahut zabardast ho. Aaj se main roz aapki personal service lungi."

Jethalal khush tha, lekin usko abhi pata nahi tha ki Lovely asal mein kitni chalak aur laalachi hai .

✦   THE BETRAYAL   ✦
Lovely Ka Sachcha Roop - Manipulation & Extortion

Agle kuch dinon mein Lovely ne pura Gokuldham Society apne jaal mein fasana shuru kar diya . Woh har ghar mein jaati, mahilayon se baatein karti aur dheere-dheere unko blackmail karne lagi . Usne har bhabhi ke saath personal baatein record kar li thi .

Lovely ne Daya, Madhavi, Anjali, Babita aur Komal sabko alag-alag tareeke se target kiya . Woh unke paas jaakar kehti ,

Lovely

"Bhabhi, aapne mujhe sirf 500 diye the... ab mujhe aur paise chahiye. Warna main sabko bata dungi ki aap log kiske kiske pati ke saath soyii hainn."

Mahilayein dar gayi . Society mein jhagde badhne lage . Lovely sabke beech mein baith kar tael daalti aur ek dusre se ladwati . Daya aur Madhavi mein jhagda, Babita aur Anjali mein baat nahi hone lagi . Pura mahoul kharab ho gaya .

✦   THE CONFRONTATION   ✦
Society Mein Bada Dhamaka

Ek din Gokuldham Society ke compound mein sab mahilayein ikatthi ho gayi . Lovely ko sabne bulaya tha . Wahan Lovely ne apna asli chehra dikha diya .

Lovely (arrogant andaaz mein)

"Aap log mujhe paise do warna main sab kuch bahar bata dungi."

Mahilayein gusse mein aa gayi . Anjali ne sabse pehle usko pakda .

Anjali

"Lovely, tu asal mein ek blackmailer nikli! Humne tujhe itna pyar diya aur tu hum sabko loot rahi hai?"

Daya

"Haan! Tu humare ghar mein aayi aur ab humein hi dhamka rahi hai?"

Lovely ne bahut drama kiya, royi, chillayi aur paise maangti rahi . Tabhi saare pati (Jethalal, Taarak, Bhide, Sodhi, Dr. Hathi) bhi wahaan pahunch gaye . Unhone pura jhagda sun liya .

Jethalal ne gusse mein Lovely ko daanta, "Tu yahan se abhi nikal ja! Warna police ko bula denge."

✦   JUSTICE DELIVERED   ✦
Lovely Ka Ant

Mahilayon ne turant police ko call kar diya . Lady Inspector aayi aur Lovely ko sab proofs ke saath pakad liya . Lovely ne bahut rona-dhona kiya lekin usko police station le jaaya gaya .

Pura Gokuldham Society rahat ki saans le saka .

✦   THE RETURN   ✦
Rukmini Ki Wapsi

Tabhi Society mein khushi ki lahar aa gayi . Rukmini wapas aa gayi thi apne pati Ganpat aur bacche ke saath .

Daya (khushi se chillate hue, Rukmini ko gale lagate hue)

"Rukmini! Tu wapas aa gayi! Ab sab theek ho jaayega!"

Saari mahilayein Rukmini ke around ikatthi ho gayi . Rukmini ne bataya ki uske pati ko wapas naukri mil gayi hai aur wo Mumbai mein hi rehne wale hain .

Rukmini

"Bhabhi log, main aap sabko bahut yaad kar rahi thi. Ab main wapas aa gayi hoon... pura ghar aur society ko sambhal lungi."

Pati log bhi bahut khush the . Jethalal ne Rukmini ko dekh kar saans li aur socha ki ab asli sukoon aa gaya hai .

Gokuldham Society phir se purani khush aur mastiyan bhari duniya ban gayi . Lovely ki yaad sirf ek buri sapne ke roop mein reh gayi aur sab log Rukmini ke saath phir se khush rehne lage .

The End
